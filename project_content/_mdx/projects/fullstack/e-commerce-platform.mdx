Create a full-featured e-commerce platform with product browsing, shopping cart, secure checkout, payment integration, and order tracking.

<h1>Project Checklist</h1>
<ul>
  <li>Design product catalog with categories and search</li>
  <li>Implement shopping cart functionality</li>
  <li>Create user authentication and profiles</li>
  <li>Build checkout process with address management</li>
  <li>Integrate payment processing (Stripe, PayPal)</li>
  <li>Implement order management and tracking</li>
</ul>

</ul>
