Create a job board platform that connects employers with job seekers, featuring job posting, search, filtering, and application management.

<h1>Project Checklist</h1>
<ul>
  <li>Create job posting interface for employers</li>
  <li>Build job search with filters (location, salary, type)</li>
  <li>Implement application submission system</li>
  <li>Add user profiles for job seekers</li>
  <li>Create employer dashboard</li>
  <li>Implement email notifications</li>
</ul>
<h2>Bonus Project Checklist Items</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Add resume upload and parsing</li>
  <li>Implement job recommendations</li>
  <li>Create saved jobs functionality</li>
  <li>Add application tracking</li>
  <li>Implement company profiles</li>
  <li>Add salary insights and market data</li>
</ul>
